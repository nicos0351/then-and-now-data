Privacy policy

Then & Now: SA Place Names · Effective 12 October 2026

In short: the app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not collect personal information, and we (the developer) receive no data about you.

Your location

If you tap Where am I?, the app asks for permission to use your phone's location. The location is used on your phone only, to find the town you are in or nearest to. It is never sent to us or anyone else, and it isn't stored. You can withdraw the permission at any time in your phone's settings; the rest of the app keeps working.

The map

The map pictures are downloaded from a map tile service (OpenStreetMap or MapTiler). As with any website, that service receives your internet (IP) address and which part of the map is being shown, so it can send the right pictures. This is covered by their own privacy policies: OpenStreetMap Foundation, MapTiler.

Town name updates

The app downloads updated place names from GitHub Pages, which receives your IP address when it does so (GitHub privacy statement). Nothing about you is sent with the request.

Purchases

Purchases (Then & Now Plus, Offline Maps) are handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was made; we never see your name, card or payment details.

Your edits and sharing

Names you record and places you add stay on your phone. Pictures and messages are only shared when you choose to share them, using your phone's own share menu.

Children

The app is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
